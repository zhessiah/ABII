## Overview

Cooperative multiagent reinforcement learning relies on information produced by teammates. A deceptive Byzantine teammate can exploit this dependence by behaving cooperatively during an initial camouflage period and switching to a harmful policy later. Because the accumulated interaction history still supports a benign interpretation, posterior intention beliefs may lag behind the teammate's true adversarial intention. Misleading teammate information can then enter latent-state representations and corrupt cooperative decisions.

**ABII** treats robust cooperation as an active Bayesian intention-inference problem. Each agent maintains continuous teammate-wise posterior beliefs over latent cooperative or Byzantine intentions. These beliefs are used not only for detection, but as an operational coordination signal that:

1. conditions latent-state abstraction;
2. gates teammate information before it shapes the filtered cognitive state;
3. rewards interactions that reduce posterior intention uncertainty; and
4. aligns information selection with long-horizon cooperative return.

The method is formulated through a **Soft-Belief Bayesian Adversarial Robust Dec-POMDP (SBAR-Dec-POMDP)** and is trained under centralized training with decentralized execution (CTDE).

## Threat Model

ABII studies **Delayed Latent Adversarial Activation (DLA)**. A Byzantine agent follows the cooperative policy before a switching time and executes an adversarial policy afterward:

<div align="center">

`cooperative camouflage` → `attack activation` → `posterior revision and robust coordination`

</div>

The latent Byzantine intention is fixed throughout the episode, while harmful behavior becomes observable only after activation. This creates a finite-time belief-lag problem rather than a conventional always-on perturbation problem.

## Method

<div align="center">
  <img src="assets/abii-framework.svg" alt="ABII framework" width="100%" />
</div>

ABII forms a closed coordination loop with four coupled components:

| Component | Role |
|---|---|
| **Bayesian intention inference** | Predicts a continuous Byzantine-risk posterior for every teammate from the agent's local interaction history. |
| **Belief-gated social perception** | Conditions a variational latent encoder on the posterior and assigns teammate-specific credibility gates before filtered-state value estimation. |
| **Active evidence acquisition** | Converts one-step posterior entropy reduction into an intrinsic reward that favors interactions producing discriminative evidence. |
| **Value-aligned robust coordination** | Uses a primary critic for posterior-conditioned policy learning and a filtered-state critic that propagates TD gradients to the information gate. |

The cooperative policy is conditioned on the local history, belief-conditioned latent state, and posterior intention belief. During training, a learned adversarial policy replaces the selected Byzantine agent's cooperative action after attack activation.

## Installation

Install the project dependencies with:

```bash
pip install -r requirements.txt
```

The repository includes configurations for the SMAC and MPE benchmark environments used by the implementation.


## Training Pipeline

At a high level, one ABII training episode follows this sequence:

```text
sample latent types and Byzantine identity
        ↓
infer posterior beliefs, gates, and latent states
        ↓
select cooperative actions
        ↓
replace the Byzantine action after DLA activation
        ↓
observe transitions and update posterior beliefs
        ↓
compute environmental + entropy-reduction reward
        ↓
update belief model, VAE/filter, dual critics, cooperative actor,
and adversarial policy
```
